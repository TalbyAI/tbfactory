## Question

Do the selected abstractions work together in the three agreed scenarios and
provide enough product value to justify the first post-MVP Factory model?

Demonstrate a provider-updated pull request with two compatible reviews and a
conflicting writer; incomplete work completed by a human before downstream
activation; and a message-originated item related to a Repository and Branch.
Use installed plugin capabilities and User-authored declarations, distinct external
and Factory state, chosen sandbox boundaries, and versioned/authorized execution.
Include one restart and one duplicate delivery. Fake provider/model effects are
acceptable when explicitly identified.

Reuse isolated prototype assets or create another self-contained integration
prototype without making production depend on them. The User evaluates behavior,
authoring, and explanatory traces. Record unmet criteria and new decision tickets;
do not close on technical execution alone.
